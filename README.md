The tradingview-selenium-fixed-time-trading-automation-bot automates your trading workflow by executing trades based on chart-driven signals. ## 🔧 Configuration Guide
After you've installed the application, you may need to configure a few settings:

1. **API Keys**: If you plan to trade automatically, link your TradingView account using API keys. This step allows the bot to place trades on your behalf. 
2. **Trade Settings**: Define your trading parameters. This includes the asset you want to trade, the trading amount, and the strategies you wish to use.
3. **Indicators**: Configure indicators as per your trading strategy. Ensure they are set correctly for optimal results.

## 📝 Using the Bot
Once everything is set up, follow these steps to use the bot effectively:

1. **Start the Bot**: Open the application and click on “Start” to begin the trading process.
2. **Monitor Trades**: Keep an eye on your trades in the console output. The bot will provide updates on executed trades and any errors encountered.
3. **Stop the Bot**: When you wish to end the session, click on “Stop.” Always ensure you stop the bot safely to prevent unwanted trades.
